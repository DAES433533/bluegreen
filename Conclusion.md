# Conclusion

Hurricane Helene was a devastating Category 4 hurricane that made landfall in September of 2024. Despite making landfall on Florida's Gulf Coast, the effects of this storm were seen spanning from Florida to the Appalachian Mountains. This cookbook provides an in-depth examination of Hurricane Helene's genesis and landfall, its coastal impacts, extratropical transition, inland impacts, vulnerability of those affected, and emergency management processes. The purpose of this cookbook is to explore all facets of a tropical cyclone impact, including social and physical. Throughout the notebooks, these facets are explored extensively, utilizing datasets including ERA5 reanalysis data, NHC best-track observations (IBTrACS), NOAA Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services (CO-OPS) dataset, Atlantic hurricane database (HURDAT2), United States Geological Survey (USGS) Water Data Website, US Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) Hurricane Helene Flood Data Collection, U.S. Department of the Interior Preliminary Landslide Inventory, and Hurricane Helene Recovery Data from the U.S. Census Bureau’s Assessment, Recovery, and Evaluation datasets. By employing these datasets, this cookbook captures the catastrophic impacts of Hurricane Helene.
